# Rulings

This file records every challenge raised during the review window and the organizer's ruling on it. The review rules are in `tournament.md`.

## How to raise a challenge

- Challenges are open only during the review window.
- A challenge is about a card model or a rules error. Strategy is not open to challenge.
- Point to the evidence: a card sheet entry, or a logged game with its game number and turn.
- Say what the official card text or the rules of Magic require instead.

## What a ruling can decide

- **Upheld:** the card model or the engine is corrected, the affected test positions are updated, and every affected deck is rerun.
- **Rejected:** nothing changes. The ruling says why.
- **Out of scope:** the challenge is about strategy or about a tournament rule, and is not considered.
